This website **does not use cookies** or any similar technology to store or read information on your device: no analytics, no advertising, no social media plug-ins and no tracking of any kind. That is why it does not show a cookie banner.

Some pages link to other websites, such as maps or the websites of the organisers. Those websites have their own cookie policies, which apply once you visit them.

If this changes, this page will be updated before any cookie is used.
