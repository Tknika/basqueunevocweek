Tknika, the owner of this website, is committed to making this website accessible, in accordance with Spanish Royal Decree 1112/2018 on the accessibility of the websites and mobile applications of the public sector.

This accessibility statement applies to the website [tknika.github.io/basqueunevocweek](https://tknika.github.io/basqueunevocweek/).

## Compliance status

This website is **partially compliant** with the standard UNE-EN 301549:2022 (which includes level AA of the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines, WCAG 2.1), due to the exceptions and non-compliances listed below.

## Non-accessible content

- The band of topics that moves across the Student Hub page cannot be paused by the visitor. It stops for visitors who have chosen to reduce motion in their device's settings (WCAG 2.2.2).
- The calendar of the event (PDF) is not a tagged document, so screen readers cannot read it in order. The same information is on the programme pages of this website.

## Preparation of this statement

This statement was prepared on 17 November 2026, from an assessment by the organisers themselves: an automated review of every page with Lighthouse (axe) and a manual review of contrast, keyboard use, headings and motion.

## Feedback and contact

If you find a barrier on this website, or need any of its content in an accessible format, write to [international@tknika.eus](mailto:international@tknika.eus). You can also use this address to send a complaint about compliance with Royal Decree 1112/2018 or to ask for information excluded from its scope.
