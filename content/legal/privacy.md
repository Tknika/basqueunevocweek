This page explains how personal data is handled on this website, in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and Spanish Organic Law 3/2018 on Data Protection (LOPDGDD).

## This website does not collect personal data

You can browse this website without giving any personal data. It has no forms and no user accounts, and it uses no cookies, analytics or tracking tools (see [Cookies](/legal/cookies/)). Its fonts and images are served from the website itself, so visiting it sends no data to other companies.

## Hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. As with any web server, GitHub may record the IP address of visitors for security and to keep the service running. GitHub explains how it handles this data in the [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Your rights

You can ask to access, rectify or erase your data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive it in a portable format, from Tknika or from its data protection officer: write to Tknika's address above, with a document proving your identity, or to [info@tknika.eus](mailto:info@tknika.eus). If you think your data has not been handled properly, you can lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority, the [Basque Data Protection Authority](https://www.avpd.eus/) (Agencia Vasca de Protección de Datos, AVPD).
