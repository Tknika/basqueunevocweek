Two parallel programmes on advanced manufacturing, from 20 to 24 April 2026.

## LCAMP Student Hub and Skill Competition

For VET students (EQF 4), 20-23 April.

| Day | Where | Activity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday 20 | Donostia / San Sebastián | Arrival of international groups, welcome and orientation |
| Tuesday 21 | IES Zubiri Manteo BHI | Team-building in international teams. Guided activity in additive manufacturing: FDM, SLS and SLA technologies, then printing and assembling a 5-piece set in groups. Free time in Donostia |
| Wednesday 22 | CIFP Santurtzi LHII | Guided activity in educational robotics: assembling and commissioning robot hardware, with electronics, sensors and a Raspberry Pi, then programming, debugging and final evaluation. Free time in Bilbao |
| Thursday 23 | Europa Congress Palace, Vitoria-Gasteiz | Skills competition evaluation and awards ceremony. Free time in Vitoria-Gasteiz |

| Day | Morning | Afternoon |
